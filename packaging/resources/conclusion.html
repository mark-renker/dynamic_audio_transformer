Installed

Rescan plug-ins in your DAW (Logic: it scans on launch; Live: Preferences > Plug-Ins > Rescan; Reaper: Preferences > Plug-ins > Re-scan). Look under Fourier_Ventures_LLC > Lovely Transformer.

Start with Drive around 1–2 and Output at 1.0; raise Drive and Even Harmonics for a thicker, more coloured sound, and use Hysteresis and Asymmetry to shape how the saturation reacts to the signal.
