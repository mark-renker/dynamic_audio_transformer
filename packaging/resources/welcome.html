Lovely Transformer @VERSION@

A transformer saturation plug-in built on a Preisach-style hysteresis model: frequency-dependent drive (the lows saturate harder than the highs, as in real iron), separate even and odd harmonic amounts, and adjustable hysteresis and asymmetry.

On the next screen tick the formats you want (Audio Unit, VST3). They go to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW afterwards; the plug-in appears under Fourier_Ventures_LLC > Lovely Transformer.

Requires macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
